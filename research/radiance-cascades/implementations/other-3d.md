# Other 3D implementations

| Primary implementation | Probe and tracer choices | Experiment to transfer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CodyJasonBennett/three-rc at 43fd4f0](https://github.com/CodyJasonBennett/three-rc/tree/43fd4f067b98b8b685259c69ff2c3eb67bf225b9) | Classic RC with screen-space probes and world-space three-mesh-bvh scene queries. The author also demonstrates a screen-space ray-marching configuration. The README identifies its depth-aware upscaler and its licensing restriction. | Keep a receiver visible and an emitter just outside the frustum. Camera yaw makes offscreen transport and screen-edge failures visible. Use as a visual and architectural reference. |
| [KingTheFifth/radiance-cascades at e196e66](https://github.com/KingTheFifth/radiance-cascades/tree/e196e66b5f90ee24fea80642d4bb09b3f1c7fac0) | Rust/OpenGL 3D implementation projects screen-space probes onto scene depth, then traces their intervals through a world-space voxelization. | At matched output resolution, place a thin wall between the emitter and receiver. Voxel size and probe spacing can be tested independently. |
| [wroughtinator/split-radiance-cascades-webgpu at 34255d3](https://github.com/wroughtinator/split-radiance-cascades-webgpu/tree/34255d334b1fc3880664d2580486bbd111f1be52) | Independent Split RC implementation with a software BVH on WebGPU. Its dynamic extension groups rigid triangles in object space, updates a top-level instance structure, and uses swept bounds for motion-local invalidation. The project describes a hinged door and a dynamic Sponza scene. | Hold a camera still and toggle the door in one frame. Check its old and new positions, the shadowed receiver and frame time. A second test moves the camera through the opening. |

The Chandra lineage has its own [port notes](port-lineage.md). Cody Bennett’s implementation is a separate classic-RC project. The independent WebGPU project's scene and timing claims belong to its particular implementation and hardware.
