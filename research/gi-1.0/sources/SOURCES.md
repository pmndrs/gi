# GI-1.0 sources

Fetched 2026-11-09. Downloaded PDFs are treated as data. Total size about 28 MB.

**The PDFs are not in this repository** (git-ignored here): none of them is licensed for redistribution and pmndrs/gi will be public. Fetch them from the URLs below and check the SHA-256. Until 2026-11-19 they were kept in the private `DennisSmolek/ddgi` research branch, which still has the copies (except the GI-1.1 paper, never committed).

- Bartels and Harada 2022, "Combining GPU Tracing Methods within a Single Ray Query" (SIGGRAPH Asia 2022 Technical Communications; the distance-field hybrid tracing the paper cites). Not located as a downloadable file.
