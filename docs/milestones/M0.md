# M0 note: foundations

Date: 2026-11-04. Branch `m0`. Measured on Windows 11, headless Chrome 156, an RTX 3060 Laptop GPU and an Intel UHD (gen-12lp). Apple devices are deferred (DECISIONS 2026-11-04). Details for each task are in [PROGRESS.md](../../PROGRESS.md); numbers are in [`bench/results/`](../../bench/results/).

## What works

- **Workspace (T0.1):** pnpm workspace, lint, typecheck, test and build. Lint stops `ddgi-core` from importing any engine.
- **GPU runner (T0.2):** `pnpm test:gpu --gpu nvidia|intel` runs test pages in headless Chrome on a chosen GPU, with screenshots and JSON results.
- **Timing (T0.3):** a GPU pass timer with a strict mode for benchmarks, and a stats overlay. Timing costs nothing measurable.
- **Scenes (T0.4):** 11 scenes from manifests, including Sponza and both Bistros (download-only, checksummed).
- **Viewer (T0.5):** scene picker, cameras, bookmarks, GI on/off, a split wipe between techniques, three's probe grid and VXGI as baselines, hover help on every control.
- **Native NVIDIA DDGI (T0.6):** our scenes run in NVIDIA's own DDGI sample (Vulkan and D3D12) from one command, with per-pass timings to CSV. Its default rendering is unchanged.
- **References (T0.7):** Cycles and a GPU path tracer agree within 0.4% on Cornell. A compare tool reports bias, block error and FLIP.
- **Shared GPU lock:** one benchmark at a time across browser, native and Cycles jobs, so numbers don't fight.

## Spike answers

- **three.js (T0.8): yes to everything.** It runs on our device, and a light node reads our textures in all three of its drawing pipelines. Turning its environment's diffuse light off inside our volume needs a workaround until three PR #35017 lands.
- **Babylon (T0.9): it fits the contract,** but Babylon always makes its own device. Babylon is now paused; three.js is the only adapter until v1 is proven (DECISIONS).
- **BVH (T0.10): we use our own tracer.** On Sponza it traces 104 M rays/s on the RTX 3060 (1.20× three-mesh-bvh) and 12.8 on Intel (1.97×), with zero leaks in the oracle. Only the watertight triangle test remains; Möller–Trumbore let up to 12% of edge rays through. Bistro exterior is 0.83×, and any-hit trails on Bistro; M1 looks at both.
- **API (T0.11): signed off by Dennis,** then revised after the spikes: a binding budget, inline tracing by default, and `range: 0` for lights without a limit ([platform.md](../api/platform.md#revisions)).

## What doesn't work yet, or isn't known

- **No DDGI of our own yet.** That's M1.
- **The sky isn't calibrated.** The viewer, Cycles and VXGI each light the sky differently, so Sponza's probe-grid half looks blue. M1 starts with one shared sky file (T1.1).
- **BVH build times:** Sponza takes 5.3 s with tinybvh, against 2 s planned. A newer Emscripten might fix it; that's Dennis's call. M1 doesn't wait.
- **Noisy numbers:** Bistro exterior's viewer baseline on the RTX 3060, and VXGI's "GPU bake time" (it misses the voxelising), aren't usable.
- **Not tested:** Apple devices, a headed browser by hand, Ctrl-C in the native scripts.
