**Terms used below:**
- **Probe:** a point in a 3D grid that stores the light arriving from every direction. DDGI updates a few probes every frame and blends each new answer into the old one.
- **Marking:** working out which probes a change affects, so those are updated first.
- **Camera volume:** a finer grid of probes that moves with the camera.
- **Recompiles:** GPU programs built while the scene runs. Each one can stall a frame, so the target is 0.

## What works

**Moving geometry (M3)**
- **Objects added, moved, removed and swapped at runtime (T3.1, T3.2, T3.7).** The three adapter notices on its own, without `markChanged`. Added or replaced meshes get a tree built on the GPU, so rays hit them from the next frame.
- **Deforming meshes (T3.0, T3.3).** Morphs, skinned meshes and the wind tree are refit on the GPU each frame they move, and rebuilt when the refit has drifted for about a second. Their poses move the GI: a folding emissive panel brightens the floor by 28.5%.
- **Probes near moved things (T3.4).** Probes in empty air near a moving object wake up and take part. The far probes a change affects are guessed from the object's size and how much light contrast it has. An object that gives off light marks every probe it can reach, as a lamp does.
- **Several volumes and a camera volume (T3.5, T3.6).** A dense volume inside a coarse one blends with no visible seam. A camera volume scrolls with the viewer and fades its moving edge over 10 spacings, so it meets the stability bar.
- **The viewer's scenarios (T3.8).** Furniture, a door, cabinets, the wind tree and a fly-through down the Bistro street play from scene files, with 0 recompiles.
- **Bistro on a 6 GB card (T3.12).** Compressed textures (GPUTex BC7/BC5).

**Materials and scenes (M4)**
- **Real textures at GI ray hits (T4.1).** Albedo, emissive and metalness come from a texture store with mip levels chosen by how wide the ray has spread.
- **Materials in the ray walk (T4.2).** See-through (glass), single-sided, alpha cut-outs, and blended surfaces passed at random in proportion to their alpha.
- **The three adapter maps every three material (T4.3),** including compressed textures (KTX2, GPUTex).
- **Every scene textured (T4.4),** with textured Cycles references for comparison.

**Fixed on the way, found by the first Mac runs:**
- **Rays slipped between triangles that share an edge, on Apple GPUs only** (2,696 of the oracle's edge rays). Dawn compiles Metal shaders in a relaxed math mode that lets the compiler fuse a multiply into a subtraction, so two neighbouring triangles could round their shared edge differently. The triangle test now puts each edge's corners in a fixed order, so both triangles do identical arithmetic. Three pages went from fail to pass.
- **Skinned meshes reached the GI one pose late.** three refreshes bone matrices once per animation frame, so our skinning compute could read the previous pose. The adapter now refreshes them first. This wasn't Mac-specific; the Mac's timing just hit it every time.
- **A morph that unfolded left the room 6-15% darker for good** (T3.4's open miss). An object that gives off light now marks the probes it lights through bounces, not only the ones that see it.
- **The fly-through started with 9 s of full-rate updates** (T3.8's open miss). The camera volume now warms up where the camera is.

## What it cost

Timings aren't gated at prototype level. Rough numbers on the M5 Pro: the Bistro fly-through runs at 60 fps with about 70-240 probes a frame and a 2-2.6 ms GI update; Sponza's probes use 58 MB at the `high` preset.

## What isn't done, or isn't known

**Known misses** (each in DECISIONS):
- Choosing the fly-through again mid-flight runs the camera volume at full rate for 3-4 s (a teleport resets the whole camera volume).
- **No GI behind see-through surfaces in the composite.** three's glass pixels carry no GI albedo, so the room seen through `glass-wall`'s pane is black in the GI term. The per-fragment path (`DDGIVolume`, the viewer) is fine.
- Deforming meshes shade with flat triangle normals at GI ray hits.
- The camera volume is the only volume within about 7 m of the camera (its 10-spacing fade).
- A point outside every volume whose nearest volume is a cold camera volume reads no bounce light (rare).
- A receiver that stops in mid-air isn't reported again, so its probes go back to sleep after 5 s.

**Not run on the Mac:**
- The door and cabinet against Cycles renders of their end states; the M4 accuracy runs left (Bistro, courtyard tree, alpha checker, sponza-full, FLIP). They need the Cycles references from the NAS (TASKS Later).
- The Intel UHD and the native harness are Windows-only. The tracer's edge fix should be re-checked on the RTX and the Intel (TASKS Later).

**Tooling:** the asset tool's recorded hashes are Windows-only, so `pnpm assets:fetch` reports the converted assets as failed on the Mac, though they load (TASKS Later).
