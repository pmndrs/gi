## What isn't covered, or isn't known

**Not run on the M5 Pro:**
- **The correctness devices.** The Intel UHD runs only on Windows. No iPad or older iOS device has been run, so the `low` preset hasn't met its intended hardware yet.
- **Accuracy against Cycles.** The references are on the NAS, so M4's remaining accuracy table and the door and cabinet end states wait for them.
- **The RTX since the merges.** The Metal shared-edge fix changes the triangle test on every backend, and should give the same bits where nothing fuses. Re-check `trace-oracle` there.
- **Safari.** Every Mac run here was headless Chrome. SPEC lists Safari on the M5 Pro as a target browser.

**Known misses, accepted under the coverage rule** (each in DECISIONS):
- Choosing the fly-through again mid-flight runs the camera volume at full rate for 3–4 s.
- The composite path draws no GI behind glass. The viewer's per-fragment path is fine.
- M2's eight known misses, accepted at M2's sign-off.
- The smaller items listed in [M3-M4.md](M3-M4.md).

**Small leftovers** are in TASKS "Later": the asset tool's Windows-only hashes, an unexported `DDGILightGroups` type, two stale code comments, and the `low` preset's warm-up batch size.

## The tuning pass: proposed, for Dennis to pick from

In the order I'd do them. Each is a separate decision, not a plan to do them all.

1. **Look first.** Dennis's look through the viewer's scenes and scenarios, and the examples site (`pnpm examples`), sets what matters. On the Bistro street: the sunlit square looks right; what reads very dark is the side street, which is entirely in the buildings' shadow, so only sky and bounce light reach it. Whether that bounce is too weak needs Bistro's Cycles reference (on the NAS).
2. **GTAO on the GI term:** keep it or turn it off by default (M3-M4 note, question 1). It moves textured scenes by 4–12 points.
3. **Correctness devices:** run `low` on the iPad and the Intel UHD, and size its warm-up batch.
4. **Safari on the M5 Pro**, as a target browser.
5. **Accuracy against Cycles,** once the references are copied: finish M4's table, then the door and cabinet.
6. **The composite behind glass,** if a host needs the composite with glass.
7. **Timing on the target GPUs** (RTX 3060, M5 Pro), back to the M1/M2 targets, only after 1–6.

The examples site and release prep are done (T5.6, T5.7, [docs/design/m5-release.md](../design/m5-release.md)): nothing is published or deployed until Dennis does the one-time steps in [docs/releasing.md](../releasing.md) (move the repo to `pmndrs/ddgi`, register npm's trusted publisher, turn on Pages). After v1: the post-v1 list in SPEC. Other GI techniques run on their own branches.
